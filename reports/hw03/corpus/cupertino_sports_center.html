Sports Center

The Cupertino Sports Center is a 25,000+ square feet multipurpose facility that features 17 tennis courts, eight pickleball courts, a fitness center with strength training and cardio equipment, free weights, two racquetball courts, indoor and outdoor sport courts, fitness classes, personal training, and a complete locker room.

The Sports Center also offers a variety of classes, camps, group, and private tennis lessons. Tennis socials and inter-club tennis is available through the Cupertino Tennis Club. Pro shop on site. Ball machine rental available.

Fitness Classes

Bombay Jam

This program has a serious focus on integrating fun and fitness! Our custom music mixes are created with the hottest Bollywood tracks and some American Top 40 music,and will motivate you instantly. Bombay Jam includes cardio and toning in one action-packed class.

Bootcamp

Get fit with this total body conditioning class combining strength, resistance, and core training for a full body transformation! Increase endurance, strengthen muscles, improve stability and balance for a strong foundation.

Dynamic Stretch and Balance

Join us for an interactive class while we build on increasing range of motion and enhance your stability.

Functional Strength Training

Join us for a dynamic workout with short and intense intervals. Enjoy the benefits of increased energy levels, confidence, metabolism, fat burning, muscle definition, sleep quality, balance, and stability.

Strength and Conditioning

Improve your muscle strength and increase lean muscle mass through resistance training. Free weights and stability balls are used to provide a variety of challenging exercises. 

TRX/Bootcamp

Join us for a functional, full body workout where we will incorporate strength and cardio intervals to strengthen and tone the body.

Vinyasa and Flow Yoga

Vinyasa links movement directly to breath, with smooth, fluid transitions you will flow through plank, cobra, and downward-facing dog. The focus will be on cardiovascular endurance and stamina. The poses are designed to improve your muscle strength, core stability, balance, range of motion, and flexibility energy.

Zumba

Zumba routines feature aerobic/fitness interval training with a combination of fast and slow rhythms that tone and sculpt the body.

Sports Center Fitness Class Guidelines 

  • Please arrive on time. There is a five-minute grace period.
  • Everyone must bring their own mat, and you must wipe down equipment before and after each use.
  • Childcare is not available.
  • Classes are open to those 18 and older.

Personal Training

The Cupertino Sports Center offers members a one-on-one experience. You can choose to train outside, in the gym, or in a private setting. Our trainers will work with you on your specific goals, whether it be weight loss, muscle development, nutrition, body transformation, injury prevention, recovery, endurance, strength, conditioning, and much more.

Pricing 

You must be a Sports Center Member to purchase personal training. Pricing is dependent on trainer certifications and years of experience. 

  • $75 to $80 - One 1-hour session
  • $350 to $375 -  Five 1-hour sessions
  • $650 to $700 - Ten 1-hour sessions

Prices are subject to change.

Start Training with a Certified Personal Trainer

Questions about personal training? Call the Sports Center at (408) 777-3160 or email sports@cupertino.gov.

Classes and Camps

The Cupertino Sports Center offers year-round classes and camps that cater to a variety of interests. Registration for classes and camps (Badminton, Basketball, Chess, Pickleball, Table Tennis, and Tennis) is available at the Sports Center, the Quinlan Community Center, and online.

For registration information, call the Sports Center at (408) 777-3160.
