Athletic Fields and Courts

The City of Cupertino supports and encourages flexible and inclusive use of athletic fields in a manner that preserves neighborhood quality of life and protects the park resource. The City supports and encourages volunteerism and the sense of community created when individuals with common goals work together. The City values the healthy benefits of recreational pursuits and supports the development of young athletes.

Tennis and Pickleball

City Park Tennis Courts

The City has tennis courts at Varian, Monta Vista, and Memorial Parks that are first-come, first-serve, and are not reservable.

Memorial Park Pickleball

Pickleball Trial July 7, 2026 through February 7, 2027
Following City Council direction from June 16, 2026, the City is moving forward with a pickleball trial at Memorial Park, part of an ongoing effort to provide accessible recreational opportunities while being mindful of the residents who live near the park. For more information on the trial as well as other coordinated steps the City is taking to better understand and manage pickleball impacts, please visit cupertino.gov/pickleballtrial.


Playing Respectfully: Quiet Pickleball Equipment Encouraged
In an effort to balance community recreation with neighborhood tranquility, the City requests players to use quiet-category paddles and balls—certified by USA Pickleball—during all hours in which play is permitted. This initiative is communicated to players by signage posted at the courts and aimed at reducing noise for nearby residents. While this is a step toward mitigating noise concerns, the City remains committed to supporting pickleball. The City continues to look into potential long-term noise reduction solutions.


Pickleball is only permitted Tuesday through Sunday, 9 a.m. to 7 p.m.

The City offers pickleball on Courts 1 and 2 at Memorial Park. Both courts are dual-striped for both tennis and pickleball.

Court 1 is available on a first-come, first-served basis for both tennis and pickleball, and pickleball has priority over tennis on Court 2. Tennis may be played on Court 2 when pickleball players are not present, but the court must be relinquished to pickleball players when they arrive. 

Click here to view the Pickleball Court Rules and Waiting System(PDF, 419KB).

For questions, please email pickleball@cupertino.gov.

Cupertino Sports Center

The Cupertino Sports Center offers a variety of sports and tennis courts which are available for rental by Sports Center members. For more information, contact the Sports Center at (408) 777-3160.

Obtaining a Permit

Athletic Field Use Permits are required for exclusive field use or for any repeated, organized use of athletic fields maintained by the City of Cupertino.

If your group is interested in obtaining a permit, or have additional questions, contact Amanda Hui at (408) 777-3374 or amandah@cupertino.gov.

Reporting Unauthorized Use

If you would like to report unauthorized use, please contact Code Enforcement at (408) 777-3182 or the Santa Clara County Sheriff's non-emergency number at (408) 299-2311.
