Softball League

Cupertino Parks and Recreation has partnered with Volo to offer adult softball, the perfect blend of competition and fun for local residents and neighboring communities. Our leagues provide a recreational atmosphere where players can enjoy healthy competition, build community, and stay active.

Prior to each season, registration for both free agents and teams is available through the Volo website. 

Check Availability and Register Here

Seasons

Three seasons are offered each year in the approximate time frames below. Registration opens approximately six weeks before the season starts and closes approximately one week before the season starts. When open, registration is available at the Volo Sports website, linked above.

Spring Season: March to May
Summer Season: May to July
Fall Season: August to October
